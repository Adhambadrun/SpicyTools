BCF floating widget

				

					The agent-side companion: a floating search panel for
					bo.bcflights.com. It detects the lead you are looking at and opens
					it in Kayak, Google Flights, ITA Matrix (including mixed-cabin and the ELR/YVR
					trick), PointsYeah or SpicyQuote — plus Sabre Fast Search, the VIP itinerary
					maker, the GK converter and the PNR how-to. Every fare on the board above
					offers the same links, built by the same module.
				

			


			

				/bcf-widget.user.js
				
			


			
    
				
  • Install it in Tampermonkey / Violentmonkey, then open any lead on Bo.BCFlights.com.
    • 
				
  • Drag the panel by its header, collapse it to the pepper bubble, hit ⟳ to re-detect.
    • 
				
  • Rebuild it after editing the sources: npm run build --workspace @spicyquote/bcf-widget.
